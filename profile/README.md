## 🌍 Who we are

We are a **volunteer-run, open community** of students, researchers, engineers, educators, and curious minds who believe that nuclear science and engineering knowledge should be free and accessible to everyone.

Nuclear technology plays a major role in the world's low-carbon energy future, in medicine, industry, and science — yet quality learning material is scattered, expensive, or locked behind university walls. We exist to change that: we build **open learning paths**, curate **free resources**, and run **community projects** that anyone can join.

No degree required. Beginners welcome.

## 🔬 What we cover

| Area | Topics |
| --- | --- |
| ⚛️ **Nuclear physics** | Atomic & nuclear structure, radioactivity, decay, fission, fusion |
| 🕹️ **Reactor physics** | Neutron interactions, moderation, criticality, reactor kinetics |
| 🔥 **Thermal-hydraulics** | Heat generation and removal, coolants, plant systems |
| ☢️ **Radiation protection** | Detection, dosimetry, shielding, ALARA |
| ♻️ **Fuel cycle & waste** | Mining, enrichment, fabrication, spent fuel, disposal |
| 🛡️ **Safety, security & safeguards** | Defense in depth, licensing, nonproliferation |
| 💡 **Applications & society** | Power reactors, SMRs, medicine, policy, public communication |

## 🗣️ Get involved

- **💬 Say hi** — introduce yourself in [Discussions](https://github.com/nuclear-community/.github/discussions) and join the conversation
- **🎓 Learn** — follow the [nuclear-101](https://github.com/nuclear-community/nuclear-101) learning path at your own pace
- **📖 Share** — suggest resources, corrections, and new content via issues or pull requests
- **🛠️ Build** — pitch or join a project in the [incubator](https://github.com/nuclear-community/projects)

Before contributing, please read our [contributing guide](https://github.com/nuclear-community/.github/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) and [code of conduct](https://github.com/nuclear-community/.github/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
